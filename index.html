About
SummerJam Founder

Exxtii

Exxtii is a Singaporean entrepreneur and creative leader building businesses and platforms at the intersection of culture, entertainment, and community.

He is the Managing Director of Recognize Studios, a creative hub dedicated to developing talent, strengthening creative infrastructure, and creating sustainable opportunities within Singapore’s street dance and urban arts ecosystem.

Over the years, Exxtii has been instrumental in organizing and developing internationally recognized platforms including Radikal Forze Jam and Summer Jam Dance Camp, bringing together leading artists, educators, and communities from around the world. His work has also extended to global platforms such as Red Bull Dance Your Style SEA, alongside consulting engagements with international dance events and festivals.

With a strong understanding of both the creative and commercial sides of the industry, Exxtii specializes in turning ideas and communities into sustainable platforms.

SummerJam Showcase Competition — Philippine Qualifier

An official franchise of RFJAM SummerJam. A 2-day event comprising a dance competition and workshops, hosted and organized by BAWZ, the SummerJam Showcase Competition 2024 champion in Da Nang, Vietnam.

RFJAM SummerJam

RFJAM SUMMERJAM is one of the largest international dance events in the Asia Pacific Region. Founded in 2015, SummerJam created SummerJam Dance Camp (SJDC) to form a larger community for dancers and choreographers.

In 2023, they branched out and introduced the SummerJam Showcase Competition (SJSC), which was held at Da Nang, Vietnam, to bring dance artists together from all over the world and share their craft. Since then, SJDC and SJSC both gained popularity with thousands of participants and audiences attending every year.

Organizer

BAWZ

Founded in 2019, BAWZ is a dance collective rooted in individuality, growth, and community. With performances on national and international stages, BAWZ has developed a distinct style that connects dancers and audiences worldwide.

Since 2022, BAWZ has hosted and organized various dance events. In 2025, it received the Ani ng Dangal Award and opened BAWZ Complex, a training home offering open classes and intensive programs.

Today, BAWZ continues to create accessible opportunities for dancers through training, mentorship, events, and community-building, working toward a stronger and more inclusive Philippine dance scene.
